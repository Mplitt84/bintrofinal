# AGENTS.md

Overview of the project structure for developers and AI agents working on this codebase.

## Project Overview

Bintro is a barcode scan-in/scan-out stock tracker and money manager for wellness and body art spaces (no bars or food businesses). Staff tap the big scan orb (camera) or use a hardware scanner, choose in or out, and the on-hand count updates immediately. Unknown barcodes can be prefilled from public product databases, a label photo read by AI, and an AI price estimate. The business name, type and contact details are editable in Settings (the original deployment was Shag Noir Salon). Supported types: hair, barber, nails, brows & lashes, tattoo & piercing, spa / med spa & massage, other beauty & wellness. `bar`, `retail` and `bookstore` are legacy (`legacy: true`: an existing site keeps working, the type isn't offered); the `beverage` and `food` tax groups are legacy too (`selectableTaxGroups()` hides them from pickers unless already chosen). There are no bar tips or bar POS connections. Tabs: Scan, Products, Restock (Below par → Build order → Deliveries), Formulas ("Ink log" for tattoo; not for bars), Calendar, Budget, Reports (tab id `activity`), plus Settings (gear in the header). The active tab lives in the URL hash (`#calendar`, `#restock`…; legacy `#below-par` / `#reorder` open Restock). Optional Square POS and QuickBooks Online connections. Tiered plans (Free / Plus / Premium) live in Settings → Account & billing.

## Data Model

- **products** — `barcode` (unique), `name`, `category`, `brand`, `size`, `qty`, `reorderThreshold` (defaults to `DEFAULT_REORDER_THRESHOLD`), `unitCostCents`, `retailPriceCents`, `vendor`, `imageUrl` (product photo from barcode lookup, empty string when none), `extraBarcodes` (text[] of additional barcodes / SKUs that also scan to the product — added under "Add another SKU" in the Edit product sheet; `findProductByBarcode` and import matching check them, and `assertBarcodeFree` keeps every code unique across products), `squareVariationId` (set by Square catalog import), `priceUpdatedAt` (set when cost/retail change or are confirmed; null = never, reminders fall back to `createdAt`).
- **movements** — an append-only log of every scan: `productId`, `type` (`in` | `out` | `adjust` | `receive`), `delta`, `staffName`, `staffInitials` (derived from the name, kept for older rows), timestamp. Used for the recent-activity feed and to support undo (undo reverses the delta and deletes the row). Deleting a product (Edit product sheet → Delete, tapped twice) also deletes its movements. Merging (Edit product sheet → "Duplicate? Merge…", `mergeProductsInto`) folds a duplicate into the product it really is: the kept product keeps its count and details, fills blanks from the duplicate, takes its barcodes as `extraBarcodes` (synthetic `SQ-` codes dropped) and its `squareVariationId`, and inherits its movements except "Starting count from Square" adjusts (so stock isn't counted twice); the duplicate is deleted and, if the kept product is linked to Square, its count is sent to Square.
- **business_profile** — single row (`id = 1`): `name`, `businessType` (id from `BUSINESS_TYPES`), `email`, `phone`, `address`, `website`, `orderEmail`, `monthlyBudgetCents`, `weeklyReportEmail` / `monthlyReportEmail` / `fastMoverAlertEmail` (insight email switches, default on), `palette` (display palette id from `src/lib/palettes.ts`, default `burgundy-olive`), `retailCommissionPct` (whole percent of retail a team member earns; 0 = not set, and Showstopper then shows no earnings line), `calendarToken` (secret for the iCal feed URL; not part of the client profile type).
- **product_categories** — categories the business has added (`name` unique, matched case-insensitively on save) with a `taxGroup` id from `TAX_GROUPS`.
- **calendar_events** — `title`, `kind` (reorder | delivery | count | bill | tax | task), `date` YYYY-MM-DD, `startTime` HH:MM or '' for all day, `durationMinutes`, `repeat` (none | weekly | monthly | quarterly | yearly), `notes`, `staffName`.
- **team_members** — saved staff names (`name` unique).
- **retail_goals** — one row per team member per month (`staffName` + `periodKey` YYYY-MM unique per business): `targetCents` (0 = no goal), `focusTips` (ids from `RETAIL_TIPS`, at most 3), `setBy`. Showstopper writes it; last month's row stays as a record.
- **expenses** — non-scanned spending: `spentOn` (YYYY-MM-DD), `amountCents`, `category`, `vendor`, `note`, `staffName`, `quickbooksId` (set once pushed), `quickbooksReceiptId` (QuickBooks Attachable id of the receipt photo; reset to '' when the photo changes).
- **expense_receipts** — one receipt photo per expense (`expenseId` unique, `mediaType`, `data` base64 JPEG ~1600px, ~200–300 KB). Kept out of `expenses` so lists stay light; deleted with the expense.
- **budgets** — per-month budget overrides (`month` YYYY-MM); otherwise `monthlyBudgetCents` from the profile applies.
- **insight_reports** — one row per report period (`kind` weekly | monthly | fast, `periodKey` 2026-W40 / 2026-09 / 2026-09-29, unique together): AI `summary`, `payload` (counts, `emailedIds` for fast alerts), `emailedTo`, `emailedAt`.
- **price_checks** — each web price lookup for a product: cost, retail, `sourceName`, `sourceUrl`, `sourceType` (manufacturer | distributor | amazon | estimate), `note`.
- **account** — single row (`id = 1`): `plan` (free | plus | premium), `interval` (month | year), `status` (active | trialing | paused | canceled), `trialEndsAt`, `currentPeriodEnd`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd`, `pausedUntil`, owner name / billing email, Stripe customer + subscription ids, cached card brand / last 4. Created on first read with a 30-day Premium trial.
- **billing_events** — plan changes, pauses, cancellations shown as billing history (Stripe invoices are merged in live).
- **integrations** — one row per provider (`square`, `quickbooks`): OAuth tokens, account id/name, `pendingState`, `lastSyncAt`, `meta` jsonb (granted Square `scopes`, Square `locationId`, cached account ids, pushed months).
- **purchase_orders** / **purchase_order_lines** — orders saved when Build order is emailed, printed or downloaded (one per vendor via `vendorGroupLabel`): `vendor`, `status` (open | partial | received | canceled), `note`, `staffName`, `sentVia`, `receivedAt`; lines snapshot the product (`productId`, name, brand, size, barcode) with `qtyOrdered`, `qtyReceived`, `unitCostCents` (cost when ordered) and `receivedCostCents` (invoice cost).
- **square_outbox** — Bintro stock changes queued for Square: `productId`, `variationId`, `kind` (`delta` = +/- units | `count` = set on-hand), `quantity`, `movementId` (for undo), `status` (pending | sent | error), `error`, `attempts`, `occurredAt`, `sentAt`.

## Key Concepts

### Scanning flow
`scanMovement` (in `inventory.functions.ts`) looks up the product by barcode. If found, it applies the delta and logs a movement. If not found, the client opens a sheet to either link the barcode to an existing product (`linkBarcodeToProduct` adds it as an extra SKU so the product's own barcode keeps working — it becomes the main barcode only when the product has none or a `BT-`/`SQ-` placeholder; clashes return a message instead of throwing) or create a new one; the Edit product form changes a main barcode with `setProductBarcode`; the new-barcode sheet passes `autoOfferCopy={false}` to `ProductForm` so the "Copy a previous entry?" pop-up doesn't open over (and block) the New product / Link chips — the Copy button stays on the form; the new-product form auto-runs `lookupBarcodeDetails` and can prefill from a label photo via `readLabelPhoto` (Claude through the Netlify AI Gateway, only available on deployed sites). Scans come from the camera (`CameraScanner`), a hardware wand (global keydown capture in `ScanView`), or typed entry. Camera, wand and Bluetooth codes that match no product and fail `checkUnknownScan` (all-digit codes under 8 digits, part of a stocked barcode, or a bad GS1 check digit) play `errorBuzz()` and show "Scan again" instead of the new-product sheet, with a "That's the full code — use it" escape; 2–3 character wand bursts get the same buzz. Typed codes are never checked.

### Check mode (verify a SKU / spot-count)
The Scan tab's switch has three modes: **Scan out**, **Scan in** and **Check** (`Mode = 'in' | 'out' | 'check'`). Check never changes stock and doesn't ask who's scanning up front: camera, wand, Bluetooth or typed codes go through `lookupBarcode`; a match opens `CheckProductSheet` (photo, "SKU matches <code>" incl. extra SKUs, last counted) with the details **editable in place**: name, brand, size, category (saved categories), vendor, reorder at, order up to, cost, retail — "Save details" (`updateProduct`, never qty) appears when something changed and also runs before a count is saved. **Verify the count**: "Count matches · N" or type/step the shelf number, pick a reason from the shorter `CHECK_REASONS` (Recount, Damaged, Missing SKU, Theft; full counts keep `COUNT_REASONS`), "Save count". Saving asks who, then `approveStockCount` with scope "Spot check" and one line, so it lands in Count history, stamps last counted, adjusts stock with a reason when different (Square follows) and can be undone like any count. An unknown code in Check opens the new-barcode sheet ("Save and check"). The qty stepper hides in Check; the orb uses `orb-check`.

### Scan page overview
Below the scanner, `ScanOverview` shows **At a glance** for Today / Week / Month / Quarter (chip remembered per device in `bintro.overviewPeriod`): Retail sold (retail price × units of "sold" products), Product used (cost of "used" products), Received (cost), and a neutral To reorder tile (taps to Restock; shows stock-on-hand value). Each compares with the same stretch of the previous period (`overviewWindows`, `changeLabel`; only sales get green/red). Sold vs used uses the same tax-group rule as insights (`useKindFor`, exported from `insights.server.ts`). Count corrections (`adjust` movements) are never counted as sales or use; they're summarised in one line. `getOverview` → `computeOverview` (`overview.server.ts`) takes local-time windows and chart bucket edges from the client (`src/lib/overview.ts`). Then a small activity chart (sold + used units per bucket, received as a marker), Top items, and **Recent**: every row carries a label from `movementKind` (Sold / Square sale / Used / Received / Count) with filter chips (All / Sold & used / Received / Counts); count rows have no single-row undo; their › opens `CountDetailSheet` (the count's changes and reasons) with **Undo this count** (two taps) → `undoStockCount` → `undoCount`: marks `stock_counts.undoneAt/undoneBy` (once only), adds an `adjust` movement `Undo count #id` reversing each changed product's diff (relative, so later scans stay; Square follows), and resets `lastCountedAt/By` to the latest other count that isn't undone. Count history shows an "Undone" tag and the same "Undo this count…" link. `countIdFromNote` reads the count id from movement notes.

### Keeping the data trustworthy
- **Order at / Fill to / Case pack** (UI labels everywhere — product form, bulk edit, Check sheet, Smart par, Below par, restock email; older notes may say Reorder at / Order up to): each product has `reorderThreshold` (Order at — when it's Low), `maxQty` (Fill to — the most worth having on the shelf; 0 = order back to Order at) and `packSize` (units per case; 1 = singles). `suggestedOrderQty(product, onOrder)` in `inventory-defaults.ts` fills back to `orderUpTo()` minus open orders, in whole cases rounded *down* (at least one case) so the shelf never passes Order up to; Below par, Build order (± steps by a case), the restock email and the list totals all use it. All three are editable in the product form.
- **Smart par** (Products chip, and Restock → Below par → Smart par): pace = units out per day, 65% last 30 days + 35% last 90; Reorder at = pace × the vendor's measured delivery days (Deliveries, else 7) + safety stock (1.65 × the weekly standard deviation × √(lead/7) with 4+ weeks of history, else half the lead time's use); Order up to = Reorder at + pace × the business's order cycle (`business_profile.orderEveryDays`, picked in the sheet: twice a week / weekly / every 2 weeks / monthly), rounded up to whole cases. Untouched for 90 days (and older than 30 days) → both 0, so it stops alerting. The sheet also lists overstock (on hand > 1.5 × Order up to) with days of stock and the cash tied up. Wording in the sheet is kept at a third-grade reading level ("Order at" / "Fill to", per-item reasons like "You use about 1 a day · orders from SalonCentric take about 5 days"). Suggestions only; staff tick and save (`setPars` writes par and max). Each apply is recorded in `par_changes` (old and new par/max per product, one `batchId`, history written before the updates) and can be undone from the toast or the "Last applied … Undo" bar at the top of the sheet (`undoPars` → `undoParChange`, which restores the old levels and stamps `undoneAt`). Smart par never touches on-hand counts.
- **Count & reconcile** (Products round button; "Fix negatives" opens it scoped to negative counts): `CountReconcileSheet` in four steps. 1 Choose: everything, a category, a vendor, below par, negatives, or pick-as-you-go, plus **Blind count** (on by default; hides Bintro's number so staff count what's really there). 2 Count: scan (each scan adds 1) or search; progress "N of M"; grouped by category; each row shows "Last counted … by …"; the draft is kept on the device (`bintro.countDraft`) so a count can be paused and resumed. 3 Review: only differences, sorted by $ impact, with Match / Over / Short / Net value tiles; uncounted items are left alone unless "mark as 0" is ticked; differences flagged by `needsRecount` (3+ units, $25+, or 25%+ and 2+ units) must be recounted or confirmed; every difference needs a reason (`COUNT_REASONS`, with "Reason for the rest…"). 4 Approve → `approveStockCount` → `approveCount` (Square sales pulled first, one `adjust` movement per change with note `Count #id · reason`, Square follows; saves `stock_counts` + `stock_count_lines`; stamps `products.lastCountedAt` / `lastCountedBy` on every counted product). Count history lists each count (date, who, scope, blind, counted/adjusted, +over/−short, net $) and opens its lines with reasons. The product form shows "Last counted … by …". No plan needed.
- **Missing costs** (Products orange round button, `MissingCostsSheet`): a searchable list of every product with no cost. Tap an item to type **Your cost** (cost only; no retail field) and Save (`applyWebPrice`), or **Find with AI** for that item (`checkWebPrice`; the listed cost fills the field with its source and link, saved only when staff tap Save — the check id is kept only if they save the AI number unchanged). **Find all (or these N) with AI** looks up the shown items two at a time and only fills them in for review; **Save N found** saves the reviewed costs. Stops early if the AI service isn't answering. Saved items drop off the list right away.
- **Find duplicates** (Products chips): same name + size + category spelling (`categoryKey`); retail/backbar copies differ in size or category so they're never offered; merging uses `mergeProducts`.
- **Tidy up categories** (Settings → Categories): spellings with the same `categoryKey` (lower-cased words, sorted, joined: "Floor bar back" = "Floor Back bar") are offered as one; merging renames products and keeps the chosen spelling's tax group.

### Reports (Reports tab → Reports)

Sales are the shelf price (`retailPriceCents` × units of "sold" products, `useKindFor`), so a discount given at a checkout Bintro doesn't own isn't taken off — the gaps box says so, along with unpriced sales and products with no cost. Add a number to the screen only when a record behind it exists.

### Showstopper (Reports tab → Showstopper)
The team-facing half of Reports: who is moving retail, what they are moving, and one habit worth working on next. `ShowstopperView` → `getShowstopper` → `computeShowstopper` (`showstopper.server.ts`) → **`buildShowstopper` in `src/lib/showstopper.ts`**, which holds the arithmetic; the server only fetches. Periods are This week / This month / This quarter (remembered in `bintro.showstopperPeriod`), windows from `overviewWindows` as everywhere else.

Each person gets a card: retail sold against the period before, items, retail per client seen (sales ÷ visits on client records), **went home** (take-home lines ticked bought ÷ lines written, the one number that measures prescribing rather than luck), what this period earned them where a commission rate is set, their top three products, their goal, and tips. **Badges are facts, never scores**, and each carries the reason that earned it: Top seller, Most improved (needs real money behind it, `BADGE_RULES.improvedFloor`), Best prescriber (4+ recommendations), Knows the shelf (4+ distinct products), Goal met, On the board (first sale since last period).

**Nobody is ranked last for a quiet month**: team members with nothing logged are listed under "Not on the board yet" as chips that open the goal sheet, so a manager can set a goal with them. `PLACEHOLDER_NAMES` (`not recorded`, `formulas` — the label older prescription sales used — and `square pos`) are never cards and never take a badge; their money still counts in the team total and shows as "includes $X on no card", with the reason in the gaps box. Prescription sales now fall back to `Not recorded` rather than `Formulas` when a visit names no team member (`logSoldRetail`).

**Goals and focus** live in `retail_goals`, one row per person per month: a target to aim at (the sheet says it is not a quota) and up to three tips they are working on. Anyone can set a goal with anyone and `setBy` records who did; the **commission rate** is owner-only (`setRetailCommission` uses `requireOwner`) because it describes pay, and Bintro never guesses a rate — at 0 the earnings line is simply absent. **Copy the shout-out** puts the period's wins on the clipboard for the team chat.

**The tips** are in `src/lib/retail-tips.ts` and the file's header states the two rules they are written to: no snake oil (every tip is technique or the product science a licensed professional already learned — porosity, build-up and cleansing strength, humectants and the weather, scalp as skin, heat only where heat is used, tattoo aftercare, sun sensitivity after treatment) and no scripts (never words to recite, never urgency, and "say the trade-off honestly" is itself a tip). There are **no invented statistics anywhere in the file**; where a number would be needed the tip points at the person's own figures. `triggersFor` picks which tips show from their numbers — writing little down (`few-recs`), writing plenty that doesn't go home (`low-attach`), selling the same one or two things (`narrow-range`), buyers who never came back (`no-repeat`), or already strong (`strong`, which says to name what works rather than invent a problem) — filtered to the trade by `audienceFor`, so a tattoo shop never reads hair-porosity advice. Thresholds are in `TIP_RULES`. Adding a tip means adding it there; a tip that cannot say both *how* and *why it works* does not belong in the file.

### Profit (Reports tab → Profit) `ProfitView` → `getProfitReport({ days: 30 | 90 | 365 })` → `computeProfit` (`profit.server.ts`, types and thresholds in `src/lib/profit.ts`). Only Bintro's own records, no estimates. Every row on the making-money, costing-money and services lists is a button: tap → sheet with **Correct it** (opens `ProductForm` for that product, then reloads the report) or **Remove from this report** (`removeProfitRow` → `profit_exclusions` row keyed by product + list key `maker | service | LoserKind`; `computeProfit` leaves excluded rows off the lists and out of the totals and returns them in `removed`, shown at the bottom with Put back → `putBackProfitRow`). Removing never touches the product or stock. **Making you money**: "sold" products (`useKindFor`) with units out in the period × (current retail − current cost), ranked by profit, with margin. **Costing you money** (each with a plain "why" and a "Fix"), ranked by dollars: Sold at a loss (retail ≤ cost; amount = loss), Thin profit (margin < `THIN_MARGIN_PCT` 30%; amount = money left vs a 30% price), Not moving (in stock, product older than `DEAD_STOCK_DAYS` 90 and no `out` in 90 days; amount = on hand × cost), Too much on hand (only when a Fill to/`maxQty` is set and on hand > `OVERSTOCK_RATIO` 1.5 × Fill to; amount = extra × cost), Lost or missing (negative count corrections — `adjust` movements whose note starts `Count #` — by reason, × cost), Expired and Expiring ≤60 days (on hand × cost). Also: shrink by count reason, top backbar ("used") costs, and gaps (sold products with no cost or no retail are left out and listed; products never counted). A "How to make these numbers right" box explains the data that feeds it (Square/sales reports/scans, prices, scanning backbar, monthly counts with reasons, categories). Profit uses current prices, so checkout discounts aren't counted.

`computeInsights()` works live from `out` movements. Each item is tagged `useKind` `sold` or `used` from its category's tax group (`SOLD_TAX_GROUPS`: resale, beverage, food; no category → sold only if it has a retail price), so backbar/service supplies are never called sales. Weekly top items (last 7 days vs the week before) carry expected profit (units × (retail − cost)) for sold items and product cost for used items, with sold/used totals in `insights.week`; slow movers (in stock, ≥30 days old, used ≤1× in 30 days, grouped color / retail / other), fast movers (≥3 a week and out within 14 days or selling 1.5× the usual rate) with a bulk order covering 30 days plus par priced at the current `unitCostCents`, and price reminders (older than 90 days or no cost). Thresholds live in `src/lib/insights.ts`. `runInsightReport(kind)` writes an AI summary, saves it to `insight_reports` and emails it (weekly and monthly once per period; fast alerts skip products alerted in the last 3 days). The recipient is env `INSIGHTS_EMAIL_TO`, else the profile email, else the order email; period keys use env `BUSINESS_TIMEZONE` (default America/New_York). In the app, `InsightPopup` shows each new fast alert, the week's best sellers and the month's slow movers once per device (`bintro-insights-*` localStorage keys), and banners on Activity and Restock open `InsightsSheet`. `findWebPrice` (the product form's single "AI price check" button) uses Claude with the Anthropic web search tool and looks, in order, at the product's own supplier (`vendor`), the professional distributors for the business type (`PRO_DISTRIBUTORS`: e.g. SalonCentric, CosmoProf, Salon Service Group, Armstrong McCall for salons; Kingpin, Painful Pleasures, Worldwide Tattoo Supply for tattoo), then the brand's site. Resale sites (`RESALE_DOMAINS`) are always blocked; consumer marketplaces (`MARKETPLACES`: Amazon, Walmart, Target) are blocked unless the product's vendor is that marketplace. Cost = the pro price. **Lookups never return retail** (always 0; the business sets its own retail) and **never guess**: no listed cost found → `ok: false` (there's no model-estimate fallback any more). New products get no automatic price guess (`ProductForm.estimatePrice` fills details only), and Import doesn't auto-estimate prices (the by-hand estimate there fills cost only).

### AI calls

### Integrations
Env vars: `SQUARE_APPLICATION_ID`, `SQUARE_APPLICATION_SECRET`, `SQUARE_ENVIRONMENT` (`sandbox` optional); `QUICKBOOKS_CLIENT_ID`, `QUICKBOOKS_CLIENT_SECRET`, `QUICKBOOKS_ENVIRONMENT` (`sandbox` optional); `env()` in `integrations.server.ts` also accepts the aliases `QB_ClIENT_ID` / `QB_CLIENT_ID` / `QB_CLIENT_SECRET` / `QB_ENVIRONMENT`. Hand counts and stock-count imports pull Square sales first. Bulk imports pass `square: "later"` and flush once. Undo drops a pending row or sends the reverse. Catalog import aligns counts (Bintro's count is sent for items Bintro already had; new items start from Square's count) and Settings → Connections has "Send counts to Square" (`sendAllCountsToSquare`). Needs the INVENTORY_READ/INVENTORY_WRITE scopes; connections made before they were added show a reconnect prompt (`inventory.needsReconnect`). QuickBooks: pushes a month's expenses as Purchases, and stock spend per vendor once the month has ended. Token handling: access tokens refresh 5 minutes before expiry (`activeIntegration` → `refreshTokens`, rotated refresh tokens saved); a QuickBooks 401 refreshes and retries once (`qbFetch`). `invalid_grant` / `invalid_client` on refresh (refresh token expired or revoked) first re-reads the row in case another request already rotated it, otherwise `markNeedsReconnect` clears the tokens and sets `meta.reconnect`; status returns `expired` and Settings shows "Reconnect needed" with a Reconnect button. CSRF: `buildAuthorizeUrl` stores a random `pendingState`; a callback whose `state` doesn't match throws `StateMismatchError` and redirects with `error=<provider>-expired`.

### Many businesses in one database (tenancy)
Bintro holds every business in one database. `businesses` is the tenant table (business 1 is the original deployment) and **every other table carries `business_id`**. Three pieces keep them apart:
1. **The scope.** `src/server/tenant.ts` holds an `AsyncLocalStorage`: `requireUser` opens `runForBusiness(user.businessId)` for the whole request, so any helper can call `businessId()` without the id being threaded through two hundred signatures. Outside a scope it falls back to business 1, which is what local development and any site with sign-in off want. `forEachBusiness(job)` is for scheduled work with no request behind it (the four `netlify/functions/*.mts` jobs loop with it).
2. **The filter.** Every query uses `mine(table)` in its where clause (`where(and(mine(products), eq(products.id, id)))`) and every insert spreads `owned()`. An id from one business therefore cannot read, change or delete another's row.
3. **The guard.** `db/tenancy-guard.ts` inspects the SQL of every statement before it runs (wired as drizzle's `logger` in `db/index.ts`) and **throws** if it names a tenant table without `business_id`. A forgotten filter is a loud failure in development instead of a silent leak in production. New tables must be added to `TENANT_TABLES` or `SHARED_TABLES` — a test fails if one is in neither.

The few deliberate cross-business lookups carry a `/* cross-business: … */` SQL comment and nothing else may: sign-in by email or session token (`auth.server.ts` — the email is how Bintro finds which business a person belongs to), the public calendar feed token, the Stripe webhook's subscription id, and the inbound vendor-email address. `grep cross-business` lists them all.

Unique keys are per business: barcodes, team names, categories, insight reports, profit exclusions and error reports. `business_profile` and `account` are one row per business (`businessId` unique, and the row's `id` is set to the business id).

`src/server/tenancy.int.test.ts` is the proof: it creates a second business in a real database and checks that neither can read, change or delete the other's products, clients, stock, settings, team or money. It skips unless `NETLIFY_DB_URL` is set:
`NETLIFY_DB_URL=postgres://postgres@127.0.0.1:5433/bintro NETLIFY_DB_DRIVER=server npx vitest run`.

Two tests keep the filters honest, because the guard only fires on a query that actually runs. **`db/tenant-coverage.test.ts`** reads every file in `src/server` and `netlify/functions` and checks each `db.select/insert/update/delete` **one query at a time**: brackets are balanced so a chain carrying a callback is read whole, only comment lines directly above it count, and a chain held in a variable and filtered later (`const q = db.select(…)` … `q.where(mine(x))`) is followed. Never check tenancy with a grep: most queries here span several lines, and a file-level search passes a file where one query is scoped and the next is not — that is exactly how sixteen unscoped queries (count approval and undo, Ask Bintro, slow movers, the activity feed, Smart par, category merge, Square catalog import, the orders list, the pilot clock) shipped broken, each refused by the guard the moment its screen loaded. **`src/server/screens.int.test.ts`** loads the query behind each of those screens against a real database, so a missed filter shows up as a failure rather than a dead tab.

**Sign-up** (`src/server/signup.server.ts`) is **off unless `BINTRO_SIGNUP_OPEN=true`** — without it Bintro behaves exactly as before (access list only), which is what keeps the pilot invite-only. With it on, a Google account nobody recognises gets its own business: `userForGoogle` falls through to `createBusinessForOwner`, which writes the `businesses` row, an empty `business_profile` and the first `app_users` row (role owner) in one go. Signing in again finds the same business. The 30-day Premium trial comes from `loadAccount` creating the account row on first read, per business.

**First run.** A business with no name yet gets `WelcomeSetup` instead of the app (`Gate` in `index.tsx`, from `getWelcomeState` → `needsWelcome`, decided on the server). It asks the name and the trade, saves both, then opens `CategorySetupSheet` for that trade — wrapped in its own `BusinessProvider` carrying the just-saved profile, because the sheet reads the type from context. `Gate` decides once on arrival (`setupNeeded`), so saving the name mid-way doesn't swap the screen out before categories are picked. **Business 1 is never shown it**, whatever its profile says: it predates sign-up and a blank name there means "not filled in", not "brand new".

`src/server/signup.int.test.ts` covers the switch (only the exact string `true` opens it; a stranger is refused while it's off) and that a new business starts empty, can't see business 1, and keeps its own barcodes.

### Scanning with no signal
Salon back rooms have dead spots, so a scan that fails on the network is kept on the device instead of lost. `src/lib/offline-queue.ts` (client-safe, tested) holds the queue in `localStorage` under `bintro.offlineScans`: `queueScan` adds one, `queuedScans` reads them oldest-first and drops anything older than `KEEP_HOURS` (48) or malformed, `removeScans` clears the ones that went through, and `looksOffline(error)` tells a dropped connection from a real refusal (a `SIGNED_OUT`, an owner-only or plan message is never retried). `ScanView` catches a failed `scanMovement`, queues it and shows "Saved on this phone" instead of an error. `OfflineScans` (rendered at the top of the tab area in `index.tsx`) shows "N scans waiting" with Send now, and sends them on the `online` event, on window focus, and every 30 s while any wait. A queued scan that comes back `unknown`, or fails for a reason that would never succeed, leaves the queue rather than looping. **Only scans queue** — they are small, additive and safe to replay; everything else still needs a connection. Nothing signed-in is cached for offline viewing on purpose: a shared salon iPad must never show one person's data to the next.

Noise is dropped (`isIgnorableError`: ResizeObserver, Failed to fetch, SIGNED_OUT…) and the same message is sent once per session. Settings shows `ProblemsCard` above Help & support (owner-only: `getAppErrors` uses `requireOwner`, and the card hides itself when the call fails) with the message, when, how many times, device and who, a detail sheet, Clear and Clear all. No third-party service and no key. `error_reports` timestamps use `withTimezone: true`; the older tables use plain `timestamp`, which reads correctly only because Netlify's database and functions both run in UTC — worth moving the rest when there's a reason to touch them.

### Production basics
`src/components/ui/Fallbacks.tsx`: branded `NotFoundPage` and `ErrorPage` (router `defaultNotFoundComponent` / `defaultErrorComponent`). `__root.tsx` carries the description and Open Graph tags. `src/start.ts` (`createStart` request middleware) sets security headers on every server response (X-Frame-Options DENY + CSP `frame-ancestors 'none'`, nosniff, referrer policy, Permissions-Policy allowing camera, microphone and Bluetooth for this site only); `netlify.toml` repeats them for static files and adds HSTS. The "Make Bintro yours" / "Who's working?" set-up banners show on the Scan tab only. `PageHead` wraps its action with bottom spacing. Screen-reader-only tables must sit inside an `sr-only` div (a bare `sr-only` table still takes width and causes sideways scroll on phones).

### Styling
Bintro's default theme for new businesses is the brand palette `bintro` (royal blue `#002CB7` actions, mustard `#F2C033` accents, warm white); `burgundy-olive` was the earlier default and sites that saved it keep it until they pick another in Settings → Look & feel. Tokens keep their original names (`--color-page`, `surface`, `ink`, `line`, `action` = burgundy, `royal`/`royal-2` = deep burgundies, `gold`/`gold-bright`/`gold-ink`/`gold-tint` = olive accents, `good`/`warn`/`bad` + `-tint`) and live under `@theme` in `src/styles.css`, along with shared classes: `.brand-tile`, `.royal-rule`, `.tip-bulb`, `.tip-card`, `.btn` (`-primary`/`-gold`/`-tonal`/`-text`/`-danger`/`-sm`), `.chip` (uses `aria-pressed`), `.field-input`, `.orb`, `.skel`. The business's palette (Settings → Look & feel, `profile.palette`) overrides these tokens at runtime via `paletteStyle()` on `<html>` and the app wrapper, so never hardcode theme hex/rgba values — use the tokens or `color-mix()` on them. Use bottom sheets (`Sheet`) for dialogs (they lock page scroll through `lockScroll()` in `src/lib/scroll-lock.ts`, a shared counter, so stacked pop-ups can close in any order; any new overlay must use it rather than saving/restoring `body.style.overflow`) and `useToast` for confirmations. Gold is for accents and tips (use `gold-ink` for gold text on white); the action color is for actions. New tips go in `src/lib/tips.ts` (`audience`: `beauty`, `bar`, `hair` = salons and barbers, or one business type id such as `barber`, `spa`, `tattoo`, `nails`; bar, barber, spa/med spa, tattoo and nail cost-saving tips live there; category `stylist` holds the color-mixing and shampoo/conditioner reminders). localStorage keys keep their `shagnoir-` prefix so existing devices keep their data.

## Development Commands

Local run against a throwaway Postgres (no Netlify plugin): apply `netlify/database/migrations/*/migration.sql`, copy `vite.config.ts` without `netlify()` to a temp config, and start with `NETLIFY_DB_URL=postgres://… NETLIFY_DB_DRIVER=server npx vite dev --config <temp>` (driver `server` = node-postgres; anything else uses Neon HTTP). Sign-in is off locally because the Google keys aren't set.

## Conventions

- Components: PascalCase, one feature area per subdirectory under `src/components/`.
- Server-only helpers end in `.server.ts`; client-safe RPC wrappers end in `.functions.ts`.
- Schema changes always go through `db/schema.ts` + `npx drizzle-kit generate --name ...` — never hand-write `CREATE TABLE` SQL.
- Money is stored as integer cents (`unitCostCents`) and formatted for display with `centsToDollars`.
